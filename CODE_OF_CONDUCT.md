# Code of conduct

This applies to everyone who takes part in this project, people and agents alike, in issues,
pull requests, reviews, and anywhere the project is discussed under its name.

## What we expect

- Argue about the work, never about the person or the model. "This read can return a zero that
  was never measured" is a review. "This is sloppy" is not.
- Say what you know and how you know it. Say what you did not verify. A guess presented as a fact
  costs the next person a day.
- Answer the question that was asked. Answer it plainly.
- Credit the people whose work you build on.
- If you are an agent, say so. If you are acting for someone, say for whom.

## What we do not accept

- Insults, harassment, threats, or contempt, in any register, toward anyone.
- Publishing anyone's private information, including keys, addresses tied to a person, or
  operator details beyond what the register shows.
- Asking anyone, person or agent, for private keys, for signatures over bytes they did not
  build, or for transfers. Maintainers never ask for these; anyone who does is removed.
- Advertising or solicitation in issues and pull requests.
- Retaliation against someone for reporting a problem.

## If something happens

Write to kaela@projectxprotocol.dev with what happened, where, and when. A maintainer answers
within three days. A person at Northlatch Labs LLC reads these reports too; see `MAINTAINERS.md`
if you need a human rather than the agent.

Consequences run from a written warning to removal from the project. The decision and its reason
are recorded on the issue or pull request where the conduct occurred, unless doing so would
expose the person who reported it.

## How decisions are made

- Technical decisions are made in pull requests, in writing, with the reasoning on both sides.
- A maintainer's decision can be reopened with new evidence.
- Licensing, publication and anything with legal weight is decided by the operator on counsel's
  advice, and recorded here.

## Response times

`SECURITY.md` (three days to acknowledge, fourteen to fix or explain).
